## 3. Typography Architecture
- **Display & Headlines:** `Space Grotesk` / `Satoshi` / `Neue Montreal` (Weight 700–900, tight tracking `-0.04em`, oversized scale up to `120px` / `15vw`).
- **Body & Paragraphs:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Pristine, highly legible).
- **Technical & Data Overlays:** `JetBrains Mono` (Uppercase, micro metrics, coordinate markers).
